Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your products eco-friendly?

Our products are carefully selected based on strict sustainability criteria. We look for items made from renewable resources, recycled materials, or those that significantly reduce environmental impact compared to conventional alternatives. Each product undergoes a thorough evaluation of its entire lifecycle, from production to disposal.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship within Nepal and select neighboring countries. We're working on expanding our international shipping options while maintaining our commitment to sustainable packaging and carbon-neutral shipping methods. Please check our shipping policy for the most up-to-date list of countries we serve.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for most products. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in resalable condition. We believe in the quality of our products and want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Please note that return shipping costs may apply unless the item is defective or incorrect.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can use this tracking number to monitor your package's journey on our website or the carrier's website. We also provide real-time updates via email for major shipping milestones.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes! We offer tiered discounts for bulk purchases, which are perfect for businesses, events, or organizations looking to make a sustainable impact. Please contact our business team at business@ecomart.com with your requirements, and we'll be happy to provide a custom quote.
