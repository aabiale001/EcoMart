Research Conclusion

Our analysis reveals that GrabNGo has established a strong foundation with its clean design and sustainability focus. However, to compete effectively with established e-commerce platforms, we recommend implementing advanced search functionality, customer review systems, and enhanced filtering options. The website's strength lies in its clear mission and user-friendly interface, which should be maintained while adding these strategic improvements.

Compared to industry leaders, GrabNGo excels in sustainability messaging but lacks some standard e-commerce features. By adopting best practices from platforms like Amazon's search capabilities and Etsy's community features while maintaining our eco-focused approach, we can create a competitive advantage in the sustainable e-commerce market.
